# Our Philosophy — Simlas Is a Companion, Not a Replacement

## Helping you play, not replacing the game

Simlas was created with a simple purpose: to help players and Game Masters enjoy *The Witcher Tabletop Roleplaying Game* without the burden of repetitive bookkeeping.

Tracking Stamina, resolving combat calculations, managing conditions, remembering durations, and maintaining character records can consume time that would otherwise be spent exploring the Continent, making decisions, and telling stories together.

Simlas aims to handle that administrative work so the people at the table can focus on playing.

**Simlas does not replace the books. It helps you use them.**

## Supporting the original game and its creators

*The Witcher TRPG* is developed and published by R. Talsorian Games, building upon the world of *The Witcher*.

Its rulebooks and supplements contain the mechanics, lore, character options, creatures, magic, adventures, and creative material that make the tabletop experience possible.

We believe those publications deserve to be supported.

Simlas is designed for players and Game Masters who use the official game materials. We encourage everyone using Simlas to obtain the relevant rulebooks and supplements from R. Talsorian Games and authorized retailers.

Simlas is not intended to provide a free substitute for those publications or to reproduce their complete contents.

## What Simlas does — and does not do

Simlas helps automate calculations, resource management, dice rolls, combat procedures, character progression records, and other administrative tasks.

It may identify game abilities, equipment, spells, Signs, conditions, and other rules elements so that its systems can process them correctly.

However, identifying or processing a game element is not the same as reproducing its complete published description.

Simlas is not intended to function as a comprehensive digital rulebook, bestiary, spell compendium, or replacement for official supplements.

Players and Game Masters should continue consulting the official publications for complete rules, descriptions, setting information, and interpretation.

## The Game Master and players remain in control

Simlas is an assistant to the table, not its storyteller or referee.

The Game Master retains responsibility for interpretation, narrative decisions, and adjudication. Players retain control of their characters, choices, and roleplaying.

Our goal is to reduce administrative friction without taking away the human creativity that makes tabletop roleplaying special.

## Respect for intellectual property

Simlas is an independent, unofficial fan-made project.

It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by R. Talsorian Games, CD PROJEKT RED, or the respective intellectual-property holders.

All relevant trademarks and copyrighted game material remain the property of their respective owners.

We aim to respect those rights by limiting public project materials to what is appropriate for the operation and documentation of a companion tool, rather than redistributing protected game content.

**Support the creators. Read the books. Let Simlas handle the bookkeeping.**
