# Simlas Tester Guide

Thank you for helping test Simlas.

Simlas is under active development. Some features are stable and live-certified, while others may be temporarily under test.

The most useful tester is not the person who tries the largest number of commands. The most useful tester is the person who reports exactly what happened.

## Before testing

Make sure:

- you are in the correct Discord server and game channel;
- you have joined the correct Simlas game;
- your intended character is active;
- your C.O.D.I. sheet is shared correctly with Simlas;
- the GM or test coordinator has told you which feature is being tested.

Useful commands include:

```text
/help
/game characters
/character show
/encounter status
/encounter participants
/turn status
/condition status
/vitals status
